Adaptations: bounded BUSY waits; lazy GPIO setup; kernel-controlled CE0; no
unwired GPIO18 power operation; explicit six-colour packing; deterministic
portrait rotation; cleanup and diagnostics. The register sequence, refresh
commands and deep-sleep command follow the reviewed (E) driver.

Bird artwork
------------
47 decorative species illustrations are included. The original nine PNGs were
cropped/background-cleaned from generated concept art for this project.
Nine further illustrations were cropped from the previously supplied labelled
Garden Ink bird sheet: rook, jackdaw, long-tailed tit, carrion crow, magpie, jay,
starling, house sparrow and nuthatch. Their labels were excluded from the crops.
The other 29 are original, programmatically drawn SVG illustrations created for
this release and rasterised to PNG. Source is in artwork_sources/; the optional
workstation build script is tools/build_extra_art.py.

These are stylised decorative representations, not garden observations,
photographs, authoritative identification plates or works attributed to a human
natural-history artist. Anatomy and plumage are simplified; age/sex variants are
not inferred from acoustic records. Do not use the illustrations as evidence of
an identification. They are not downloaded from third-party bird publishers.

The runtime manifest includes exact scientific-name mappings and several aliases.
Custom PNGs retain their owners'
terms. No external images are fetched by the running application.

Font and package dependencies
-----------------------------
Font binaries are not included. install.sh installs DejaVu through the OS
package manager; Pillow, spidev and gpiozero are likewise external distribution
packages and retain their respective licences. This archive is not an operating
system image or a universal offline bundle of compiled system dependencies.

OpenObservatory
---------------
The client was written against the public API in simonjgreen/OpenObservatory at
commit d71e77d0339188793df6b4a5305faf6f7c82d2c5. This is a separate client; no
OpenObservatory source files, recordings, private station configuration or access
tokens are bundled. Protocol/source references are in docs/API_CONTRACT.md.
